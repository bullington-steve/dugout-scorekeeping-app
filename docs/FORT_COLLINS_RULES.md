# City of Fort Collins Adult Softball Rules (as supplied by Steve)

Source: City of Fort Collins Adult Softball Rules, updated May 2026. Anything not listed follows USSSA Softball Rules. This is a summary of the parts that matter to ScoreKept. Built-in rule sets cite this source.

## Game rules (all divisions)

- Minimum 8 players to start. No maximum in the lineup. Everyone on the lineup card bats (no designated hitters). Subs must notify the umpire; a player subbed out can't re-enter; a sub bats in the replaced player's spot. Late arrivals go to the bottom of the order. A player leaving without a replacement is an out when their spot comes up. Injury: under 10 players no automatic out; 11+ it's an out unless a sub is used.
- Game starts at the scheduled time with a **coin toss** for home team.
- **55-minute time limit or 7 innings**, whichever first.
- **Run rule:** 20 after 3, 15 after 4, 10 after 5. No flip rule.
- **Tie-breakers:** regular season, 1 extra inning then a tie. In the extra inning, the **last legal batter from the previous inning starts on 2nd**; no courtesy runner for them. Playoffs: unlimited extra innings.
- **Batting:** start 0–0. **3 balls = walk, 2 strikes = out.** No courtesy foul after the first strike (a foul on strike two is an out). Bunting = out. Stepping out of the box = out.
- **Pitching:** slow pitch underhand, 3–10 ft arc; illegal pitch is a ball unless the batter swings.
- **Courtesy runner:** 1 per inning per gender (unlimited in Senior). Must be in the lineup and not currently due up; if on base when their turn to bat comes, the batter is out.
- Field: 70 ft bases, 50 ft pitching (optional 6 ft back). USSSA 1.20 BPF bats. Classic M (men) / Classic W (women) balls.

## Mixed (coed) league modifications

- **Batting order:** men may not exceed women/non-male players by more than one (6:5, 5:4 legal; 6:4, 7:5 illegal). No alternating requirement.
- **Defense:** men may not exceed women/non-male players.
- Men hit a 12" ball; women/non-male choose 11" or 12" for the whole game.
- **Walk rule:** 3 straight balls without a strike, or an intentional walk, awards the batter **2nd base**; the next batter may bat or walk.
- **Commit line** (also Senior): once past the line between third and home the runner can't return, must touch the safety base to score (touching home = out); the fielder must touch home for the out (tagging the runner = safe).

## Park and other notes (League Notes only)

- No alcohol, no glass, leashed dogs, no hitting into fences. Speakers allowed, no explicit lyrics, volume at the umpire's discretion.
- Lightning delay up to 20 minutes. Weather line 970-416-2887, teamsideline.com/fortcollins. Games not reaching the end of the 4th or 40 minutes are replayed in full.
- Protests: rule interpretation only; umpire has final say. Conduct and ejection rules in the full rulebook.
